## Research Question
How can overestimation bias in actor-critic methods be systematically addressed for continuous action domains?

## Algorithm Summary
TD3 introduces three improvements over DDPG: (1) clipped double Q-learning using the minimum of two Q-networks, (2) delayed policy updates where the actor is updated less frequently than the critic, and (3) target policy smoothing that adds noise to target actions. Together these techniques significantly reduce overestimation and improve stability over DDPG.

## AutoMarkov Mapping
- Twin critic architecture relates to checkpoint versioning in `policy_export.py`.
- Delayed update cycle can be configured in `rllib_training.py` training frequency parameters.
- Target policy smoothing noise connects to exploration noise in `generation_methods.py`.

## Benchmarks
- MuJoCo continuous control suite
- OpenAI Gym continuous tasks
- PyBullet locomotion benchmarks

## Limitations
- Still on-policy (collects with current policy), less sample efficient than SAC.
- Sensitivity to exploration noise scale.
- Target smoothing can slow convergence in deterministic environments.
